The eastern extension has been moving through the approval process since September. Updates are listed newest first.

## 29 Sep 2026 · Board approves the extension

The board cleared the route with **two changes** to station locations:

- The Eastern Market stop moves 400 m north
- A new interchange is added at the ring road

> Work is expected to start after the budget review.

[Source](https://example.com/metro-approved)

## 20 Sep 2026 · Public hearing scheduled

Residents can submit objections until the end of the month.

## 12 Sep 2026 · Route proposal sent to the state board

The planning body submitted the revised route for the eastern extension.
